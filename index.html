A vision for the narrow road

The Call

Households gathered, disciples made, God worshiped openly. What I long to see, where we are now, and the part I'm called to play.

Vision My Part

The dream

Every American who is to be saved, walking the narrow road God has given and worshiping Him wholeheartedly. Matthew 7:14

  • Vibrant familiesHomes full of character, love, sacrifice and diligence.
  • Gathered householdsHouseholds gathered together so parents can raise healthy children who love and worship God. Deuteronomy 6:6–7
  • Sent outwardFamilies reaching people overseas too, leading them to saving faith in and knowledge of Jesus Christ. Matthew 28:19–20
  • Mature, shepherd-hearted peopleSelf-sacrificing, well off, caring well for their own families and deeply caring for others. Leo is the picture of this. 1 Peter 5:2–3
  • TenaciousAble to endure hardship and persecution without shrinking back. 2 Timothy 3:12
  • A united churchBelievers standing together as Protestants on one front. John 17:21
  • Love without divisionToday's confused ideologies gone, and people simply loving one another in Christ. John 13:35
  • Public worshipGod worshiped and glorified openly, in public spaces.
  • A fragrance to the LordLives that rise to God as a pleasing aroma. Ephesians 5:2

The gap

Where we are now.

  • Quiet, comfortable, selfish livesMany people live for themselves and rarely think about others.
  • Theologically shallowPeople adopt whatever someone with a microphone says instead of studying the Word themselves. Institutions say "study for yourself" but rarely equip people to do it. Acts 17:11
  • Formed by nonsenseShaped by empty and false ideas rather than by Christ.
  • Not growing upFewer people becoming Christlike, honorable men and women.
  • Hunkered downFear, anxiety and self-protection instead of courage and sacrifice.

The longing

  • From fearful and comfortable to bold and self-giving.
  • From formed by the culture to formed by Christ.
  • From borrowed opinions to knowing the Word for themselves.
  • From scattered believers to gathered households and a united church.
  • From private faith to public worship.
  • From self to others, and from the neighborhood to the nations.
For we are to God the pleasing aroma of Christ among those who are being saved and those who are perishing. 2 Corinthians 2:15

What I bring

  • PrayerI can pray.
  • Making disciplesI can meet with people and invest in them.
  • TeachingI can teach people.
  • TechnologyI can use technology to help people.
  • ManagementI have managerial abilities.
  • Social media connectionsFor some reason, God has put me among these connections.

Where I need to grow

  • Next stepsAfter meeting with someone, I'm not clear how to help them take a next step.
  • Keeping peopleI can teach, but people don't stay connected.

Convictions

  • Shepherd, not itinerant speakerI want my own disciples, to shepherd and care for people myself: hands-on, on the ground, going after revival.
  • Keep studyingEven with a master's, keep studying and growing.
  • Accountability mattersHouse churches can be too loose, removing people from accountability and letting the ungrounded lead others.

Social media ministry

A vehicle, not the destination. Two things to settle first:

  1. Purity. Prepared and above reproach before taking on a public platform. 1 Timothy 4:12
  2. Overseas? Whether God is calling me to go overseas or not.

Open questions

  • How does an online platform feed into real, local, accountable discipleship rather than replacing it?
  • What simple path can I walk someone through, from first meeting to maturity to making disciples themselves?
