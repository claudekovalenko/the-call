# My Part in The Call

*Personal discernment notes: what I bring, where I need to grow, and the questions I'm still working through.*

## What I bring

- **Prayer.** I can pray.
- **Making disciples.** I can meet with people and invest in them.
- **Teaching.** I can teach people.
- **Technology skills.** I can use technology to help people.
- **Management.** I have managerial abilities.
- **Social media connections.** For some reason, God has put me among these connections.

## Where I need to grow

- **Next steps.** After I meet with someone, I'm not clear on how to help them take a next step.
- **Keeping people.** I can teach, but I don't keep people well. They don't stay connected.

## Convictions about how I want to serve

- **Shepherd, not itinerant speaker.** Becoming a speaker who travels around just talking would be a failure. I want my own disciples and to shepherd and care for people myself: hands-on, on the ground, going after revival.
- **Keep studying.** Even with a master's, I want to keep studying and growing.
- **Accountability matters.** I'm not a big fan of the house church model. It isn't unbiblical, but it often feels too loose. It can remove people from accountability, and people without grounding end up leading others.

## Social media / online ministry

Social media might be a vehicle: a means to an end, not the end itself. I'd feel fairly comfortable starting an online work, especially after finishing a master's.

Two things to settle first:

1. **Purity.** Being prepared and above reproach before taking on a public platform (1 Timothy 3:2; 4:12).
2. **Going overseas?** Whether God is calling me to go overseas or not.

## Open questions

- How does an online platform feed into real, local, accountable discipleship, rather than replacing it?
- What is a simple, clear path I can walk someone through, from first meeting to maturity to making disciples themselves?
