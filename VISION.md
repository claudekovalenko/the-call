# The Call — Vision

## The dream

Every American who is to be saved, walking the narrow road God has given (Matthew 7:14), worshiping Him wholeheartedly.

- **Vibrant families.** Homes full of character, love, sacrifice and diligence.
- **Sent outward.** Those families reaching people overseas too, leading them to saving faith in and knowledge of Jesus Christ (Matthew 28:19–20).
- **Mature, shepherd-hearted people.** Self-sacrificing, well off, caring well for their own families and also deeply caring for others (1 Peter 5:2–3). Leo is the picture of this.
- **Tenacious.** People able to endure hardship and persecution without shrinking back (2 Timothy 3:12; James 1:2–4).
- **Love without division.** Places where today's confused ideologies (what we'd call "wokeness") are gone, and people simply love one another in Christ (John 13:35).
- **Gathered households.** Households gathered together so parents can raise healthy children who love and worship God (Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Ephesians 6:4).
- **A united church.** Believers standing together as Protestants on one front instead of scattered and divided (John 17:21; Ephesians 4:3–6).
- **Public worship.** God worshiped and glorified openly, in public spaces, not just behind closed doors.
- **A fragrance to the Lord.** Lives that rise to God as a pleasing aroma (2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2).

## The gap: where we are now

- **Quiet, comfortable, selfish lives.** Many people live for themselves and rarely think much about others.
- **Formed by nonsense.** People take in a steady stream of empty and false ideas, and they're shaped by those ideas rather than by Christ.
- **Not growing up.** Fewer people are becoming Christlike, honorable men and women.
- **Theologically shallow.** People don't think for themselves. They adopt whatever someone with a microphone says instead of studying the Word themselves (Acts 17:11). Institutions tell people to study the Bible for themselves, but rarely equip them to actually do it. Frameworks can help people understand, but without real study people end up believing foolish things.
- **Hunkered down.** Instead of courage and sacrifice, there's fear, anxiety and self-protection.

## The longing

From fearful and comfortable to bold and self-giving.
From formed by the culture to formed by Christ.
From borrowed opinions to people who know the Word for themselves.
From scattered believers to gathered households and a united church.
From private faith to public worship.
From self to others, and from the neighborhood to the nations.

> "For we are to God the pleasing aroma of Christ among those who are being saved and those who are perishing." — 2 Corinthians 2:15
